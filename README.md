## Software Engineering
### Job Application System
- Started - October 04, 2026
### General Objective

- To design and develop a Job Portal System that connects employers and job seekers, allowing employers to post and manage job vacancies while applicants search for jobs and apply, with built in tools for screening, interviewing, hiring, and reporting.

### Specific Objectives

- To build a relational database that properly connects employers, job posts, applicants, applications, screening, interviews, and hiring records.

- To allow employers to register, create a profile, and manage job vacancies from posting through closing or filling the position.

- To allow applicants to register, build a profile with their education and experience, search for jobs, and submit applications.

- To automate the applicant screening process through a standardized, weighted scoring system rather than manual judgment alone.

- To support the full hiring workflow, from shortlisting through interview scheduling, evaluation, and final candidate selection.

- To automatically update related records (application status, job status) when a hiring decision is made, keeping data consistent across the system.

- To generate reports and a dashboard that summarize job postings, applications, screening outcomes, and hiring statistics for decision making.

- To implement authentication and role based access control, so employers, applicants, administrators, HR staff, and managers each only access what their role permits.

- To apply modular system design, where each part (employer module, applicant module, screening module, and so on) functions independently but integrates into one complete workflow.
